I'm quitting LLMs for development.

- It turns programming into a slot machine
- Prevents you from learning deeply and using your brain
- Until the models get good enough to do _everything_, relying on them literally constrains
  the projects you try to complete
- It can trick you into thinking you know more than you do

So far, I've found that I don't really miss LLMs - perhaps I'm being old fashioned, but there's
less of an impedance mismatch with how I naturally work. I still have to test whether I'm 
able to use LLMs more effectively once I return to them, having gained more knowledge about 
how to do engineering the old-fashioned way.
