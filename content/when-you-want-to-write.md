When you want to think, write.
When you want to write, publish. 
When you want to publish, ask for feedback.
When you want to ask for feedback, incorporate feedback you've been given. 
When you want to incorporate feedback, put it into practice.
When you want to put it into practice, start performing.
When you want to perform, get paid.
When you want to get paid, grind.
When you want to grind, keep grinding.
