How do you get more done? Thinking takes less effort than action, but action has the benefit of 
accomplishing something. Therefore, if a domain is well understood, it sometimes makes more sense
to do the thing than it does to keep mulling it over.

To this end, it's probably a good idea to turn every single "I should" into a decision process.

1. Do it now, or reschedule to a future date where I have more information.
2. After rescheduling to a future date, either do it immediately and add it to the done list, or 
  satisfy myself with never doing it.
